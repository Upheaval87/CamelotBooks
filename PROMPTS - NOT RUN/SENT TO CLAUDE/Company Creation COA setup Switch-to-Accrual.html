Accounting method — how this company keeps its books

Accrual (Recommended)
Record income when earned & expenses when incurred. Tracks receivables, payables, inventory, accruals & prepayments. Full Balance Sheet + Income Statement. Best for credit, inventory, loans, external reporting.
Cash
Record only when cash moves. Simpler, smaller chart. Best for tiny cash-only businesses. You can switch to accrual later via a controlled conversion.
⚠ What changes in your books: accrual adds AR / AP / inventory / accrual / prepayment accounts and invoice & bill modules; cash omits them and reports cash in/out. The method is stored per company and inherited by the Chart of Accounts.
Reporting preference: Accrual view · cash-basis view available as a report toggle
