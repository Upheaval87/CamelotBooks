## 1. PURPOSE

1.1 This Information and Communication Technology (ICT) Policy ("the POLICY") establishes the rules, standards, and procedures governing the use, management, and protection of all ICT resources owned, managed, or administered by Ufulu Finance Limited ("Ufulu Finance").

1.2 The POLICY is designed to:

(a) Ensure the integrity, reliability, availability, and optimal performance of all IT systems supporting the Ufulu's business operations;

(b) Protect Ufulu Finance's information assets, member data, financial records, and confidential information from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction;

(c) Ensure compliance with applicable laws, regulations, cooperative society legislation, data protection requirements, and financial regulatory standards;

(d) Establish clear standards of conduct for all users of Ufulu Finance's ICT resources;

(e) Define appropriate sanctions and disciplinary procedures for non-adherence;

(f) Establish a framework for cybersecurity, incident response, disaster recovery, and business continuity;

(g) Ensure proper governance, accountability, and oversight of all ICT activities.

1.3 This POLICY supersedes and replaces the Ufulu Finance ICT Policy 2019 (Version 1.0) in its entirety.

---

## 2. SCOPE

2.1 This POLICY shall apply to:

(a) All IT systems, hardware, software, networks, and infrastructure owned, managed, leased, or administered by Ufulu Finance;

(b) All employees, contract workers, interns, consultants, third-party vendors, and any other persons ("USERS") who access Ufulu Finance's IT systems, data, or network;

(c) All forms of data stored, processed, or transmitted through Ufulu Finance's IT systems, including but not limited to member records, financial transactions, loan data, accounting records, employee records, and operational data;

(d) The use of email, internet, cloud services, mobile devices, and any other electronic communication tools provided by or used in connection with Ufulu Finance's business;

(e) All physical locations where Ufulu Finance IT equipment is deployed, including head office, branches, and remote working locations.

2.2 The EMPLOYEE may be:

(a) Required to, or in the furtherance of duties, use or gain access to the IT SYSTEM ("the USER");

(b) Authorized to gain access to parts of the IT SYSTEM which are not normally accessible to general USERS ("the AUTHORISED USER").

2.3 This POLICY covers both organization-owned and personally-owned devices used to access Ufulu Finance systems or data.

## 4. IT GOVERNANCE

### 4.1 Governance Principles

4.1.1 All IT systems, infrastructure, and services must be approved, managed, and maintained by or under the authority of the IT Department.

4.1.2 All employees and authorized users must comply with approved IT policies, procedures, standards, and guidelines.

4.1.3 No employee or third party may introduce unauthorized hardware, software, applications, network devices, or cloud services into Ufulu Finance's IT environment without prior written approval from the IT Department.

### 4.2 IT Asset Register

4.2.1 All IT assets must be recorded in the **IT Asset Register** maintained by the IT Department (see Section 24).

4.2.2 The IT Asset Register must be reviewed and updated at least quarterly.

### 4.3 Change Management

4.3.1 All significant changes to IT systems, infrastructure, applications, or configurations must follow the approved **Change Management Process** (see Section 22).

4.3.2 No changes to production systems shall be made without documented approval and testing.

### 4.4 System Ownership

4.4.1 Every critical system must have a documented **System Owner** (a business stakeholder responsible for the system's function) and a **System Administrator** (an IT professional responsible for its technical operation).

4.4.2 The System Owner and System Administrator roles shall be documented and reviewed annually.

### 4.5 Periodic Reviews

4.5.1 IT management shall periodically review (at least semi-annually):

(a) System performance and availability;

(b) Security posture and compliance with this POLICY;

(c) User access rights and privileges;

(d) Backup and recovery readiness;

(e) Software licensing and currency;

(f) Vulnerability and patch management status;

(g) Incident trends and lessons learned.

4.5.2 A summary of IT governance findings shall be reported to senior management and, where appropriate, to the Board or relevant Board Committee.

### 4.6 Segregation of Duties

4.6.1 IT functions and business functions shall be segregated to prevent conflicts of interest and unauthorized activities.

4.6.2 System administrators shall not hold business transaction processing rights in financial systems unless explicitly authorized and documented.

4.6.3 Development, testing, and production environments shall be separated.

(b) Develop and enforce IT procedures, standards, and guidelines in support of this POLICY;

### 5.4 Heads of Department / Section

(a) Comply with this POLICY and all related IT procedures;

(b) Use IT resources responsibly and only for authorized business purposes;

(c) Protect their login credentials and never share passwords;

(d) Report security incidents, suspicious activity, and policy violations promptly;

(e) Complete required IT security training;

(f) Lock workstations when unattended;

(g) Follow proper procedures for data handling, storage, and disposal.

### 5.6 Internal Audit

(a) Periodically audit IT controls, access logs, and compliance with this POLICY;

(b) Review financial system audit trails and transaction integrity;

(c) Verify backup and disaster recovery readiness;

(d) Report audit findings and recommendations to management and the Board.

## 6. ACCEPTABLE USE

### 6.1 General Principles

6.1.1 The IT SYSTEM shall only be used for its intended purposes, consistent with the functions USERS are required to perform in furtherance of Ufulu Finance's BUSINESS.

6.1.2 All users are responsible for their actions and activities when using Ufulu Finance's IT resources.

### 6.2 Prohibited Uses

The IT SYSTEM may **not** be used for:

(a) Commercial purposes unrelated to the BUSINESS of Ufulu Finance;

(b) The creation, promotion, transmission, receipt, storage, or viewing of offensive, pornographic, discriminatory, harassing, or otherwise inappropriate material;

(c) Any illegal, fraudulent, or unauthorized activity;

(d) Any purpose inconsistent or conflicting with the contractual obligations of Ufulu Finance, or any software or licensing agreement relating to the IT SYSTEM;

(e) Personal financial gain or unauthorized commercial activities;

(f) Downloading, distributing, or executing malicious software, viruses, worms, trojans, or ransomware;

(g) Attempting to gain unauthorized access to any system, data, or network resource;

(h) Circumventing or attempting to circumvent security controls, access restrictions, or monitoring systems;

(i) Cryptocurrency mining or any activity that excessively consumes organizational computing resources without authorization.

### 6.3 Monitoring

6.3.1 Ufulu Finance reserves the right to monitor, log, and review all activity on its IT systems, including but not limited to email, internet usage, file access, application usage, and network traffic, for purposes of ensuring compliance with this POLICY, protecting organizational assets, and meeting legal or regulatory obligations.

6.3.2 USERS acknowledge and accept that:

(a) Ufulu Finance does not guarantee nor undertake to protect the confidentiality or privacy of any communication to or by the USER on the IT SYSTEM, nor any activity of the USER;

(b) All email, internet, and other communications or activities by the USER on the IT SYSTEM may be monitored and recorded by the IT Department;

(c) Ufulu Finance shall be entitled to use any information obtained from monitoring the USER's activities on the IT SYSTEM for purposes of enforcing this POLICY;

(d) Ufulu Finance shall be entitled to disclose any information so obtained to relevant authorities if, in Ufulu Finance's sole discretion, it considers the USER to have been involved in any illegal or criminal activity whilst using the IT SYSTEM.

## 7. USER ACCOUNTS & ACCESS CONTROL

### 7.1 Individual User Accounts

7.1.1 Every employee and authorized user must have an individual, uniquely identified user account.

7.1.2 Generic, shared, or anonymous user accounts are prohibited except where specifically approved and documented by the IT Department for a defined technical purpose.

7.1.3 Sharing of usernames and passwords between employees or with any other person is strictly prohibited.

### 7.2 Least-Privilege Principle

7.2.1 Users must only receive the minimum level of system access required to perform their specific job responsibilities.

7.2.2 Access rights shall be determined based on the user's role, department, and approved job requirements.

7.2.3 Privileged and administrator accounts must be restricted to specifically authorized IT personnel and must be subject to enhanced monitoring and controls.

### 7.3 Access Request and Approval

7.3.1 All requests for new user accounts or changes to existing access rights must be submitted in writing (using the prescribed **User Access Request Form** – Appendix A) and approved by:

(a) The requesting employee's Head of Department; and

(b) The System Owner or designated business approver for the relevant system.

7.3.2 The IT Department shall provision access only upon receipt of a properly approved request.

### 7.4 Periodic Access Review

7.4.1 User access rights, including privileged accounts, shall be reviewed at least **semi-annually** by the IT Department in conjunction with relevant System Owners and Heads of Department.

7.4.2 Access rights that are no longer required shall be revoked promptly.

### 7.5 Account Termination and Dormancy

7.5.1 Accounts for employees who leave Ufulu Finance (whether by resignation, termination, retirement, or otherwise) must be disabled or removed **on or before the employee's last working day**.

7.5.2 The Human Resources Department must notify the IT Department of all employee separations, transfers, and role changes in a timely manner.

7.5.3 Dormant accounts (accounts with no login activity for a period exceeding **90 days**) shall be automatically disabled by the system. Re-activation requires a new approval request.

7.5.4 Accounts for contractors and third-party personnel shall be disabled immediately upon expiration of their engagement.

### 7.6 Multi-Factor Authentication

7.6.1 Multi-Factor Authentication (MFA) shall be enabled for:

(a) Access to critical financial systems and the core banking application;

(b) Remote access and VPN connections;

(c) Administrative and privileged accounts;

(d) Email and cloud-based services where technically feasible;

(e) Any system identified by the IT Department as high-risk.

### 7.7 Access to Restricted Systems

7.7.1 Restricted parts of the IT SYSTEM, as determined by Ufulu Finance from time to time, shall only be accessible to AUTHORISED USERS.

7.7.2 Access to production databases, server infrastructure, network equipment, and financial system configuration settings shall be strictly limited to designated AUTHORISED USERS.

## 8. PASSWORD & AUTHENTICATION

### 8.1 Purpose

This section is designed to protect Ufulu Finance resources by requiring strong passwords, proper credential protection, and periodic password changes.

### 8.2 Password Protection Rules

All users must adhere to the following rules:

(a) Never write passwords down or store them in unencrypted form;

(b) Never send a password through email, instant messaging, or any unsecured channel;

(c) Never include a password in an unencrypted stored document;

(d) Never tell anyone your password, including colleagues, supervisors, IT staff, or vendors;

(e) Never reveal your password over the telephone;

(f) Never hint at the format or content of your password;

(g) Never reveal or hint at your password on any form, website, or questionnaire;

(h) Never use the "Remember Password" feature in applications, browsers, or email clients on shared or organizational computers;

(i) Never use your Ufulu Finance corporate or network password on any external internet account or service that does not use a secure connection (https://);

(j) Report any suspicion that your password has been compromised to the IT Department immediately;

(k) If anyone asks for your password, refer them to the IT Department;

(l) Do not use common acronyms, dictionary words, reverse spellings, names of people or places, or parts of your login name in your password;

(m) Do not use easily guessable numbers such as phone numbers, ID numbers, dates of birth, or street addresses;

(n) Be careful to prevent others from seeing you type your password (shoulder surfing).

### 8.3 Password Requirements

The following password standards shall be enforced by the IT Department (subject to periodic review):

| Parameter | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Minimum Length | 8 characters (12 characters recommended for privileged accounts) |
| Maximum Length | 30 characters |
| Complexity | Must include at least three (3) of the following four character types: (i) Lowercase letters (a–z), (ii) Uppercase letters (A–Z), (iii) Numbers (0–9), (iv) Special characters (e.g., !@#$%^&*(){}[]) |
| Dictionary Words | No complete dictionary words permitted |
| Case Sensitivity | Passwords are case sensitive |
| Password History | Last 10 passwords cannot be reused |
| Change Frequency – Core Banking System (Finfinancials) | Every 30 days |
| Change Frequency – PC/Network User Accounts | Every 90 days (4 months) |
| Change Frequency – Privileged/Administrator Accounts | Every 60 days |
| Account Lockout Threshold | 3 consecutive failed login attempts |
| Account Lockout Duration | 30 minutes or manual unlock by IT |
| Screen Saver Lock | Password-protected screen saver activating within 5 minutes of user inactivity |

### 8.4 Default and Initial Passwords

8.4.1 All default passwords (on hardware, software, appliances, and systems) must be changed before any system is put into production.

8.4.2 Initial/temporary passwords issued to new users must be changed at first login.

### 8.5 Password Managers

8.5.1 Where appropriate and approved by the IT Department, an enterprise password manager may be used to generate and store complex passwords securely.

8.5.2 Personal password managers or browser-based password storage are not approved for storing organizational credentials.

### 8.6 Password Reset Procedures

8.6.1 Password reset requests must be verified through an approved identity verification process before the reset is performed.

8.6.2 Password resets shall be performed only by authorized IT personnel.

8.6.3 Temporary passwords issued during a reset must be changed by the user at next login.

### 8.7 Administrator Passwords

8.7.1 Administrator and root-level passwords must be protected with the highest level of security.

8.7.2 Administrator passwords shall not be shared unless absolutely necessary and must be stored in a secure, access-controlled manner (e.g., a sealed-envelope procedure or enterprise password vault).

8.7.3 Administrator passwords shall be changed immediately after any personnel change in the IT team or after a suspected compromise.

### 8.8 Enforcement

Employees who do not adhere to the password and authentication requirements may be subject to disciplinary action.

## 9. COMPUTER & DEVICE SECURITY

### 9.1 Business Use

9.1.1 Organization-owned computers, laptops, and devices are provided primarily for authorized business purposes.

9.1.2 Limited, reasonable personal use may be permitted provided it does not interfere with work duties, violate any provision of this POLICY, or compromise security.

### 9.2 Workstation Security

9.2.1 Users **must lock their computers** (Windows Key + L or equivalent) whenever leaving their workstation, even briefly.

9.2.2 Users must not leave their computers unlocked and unattended.

9.2.3 Password-protected screen savers must be enabled and configured to activate within **5 minutes** of inactivity.

### 9.3 Security Controls

9.3.1 Users must not disable, bypass, tamper with, or interfere with:

(a) Antivirus or endpoint protection software;

(b) Firewall software or hardware;

(c) Intrusion detection or prevention systems;

(d) Disk encryption;

(e) Any other security control installed or configured by the IT Department.

### 9.4 Removable Media and USB Devices

9.4.1 Unauthorized USB storage devices, external hard drives, and other removable media shall not be connected to organizational computers without IT Department authorization.

9.4.2 Where use of removable media is authorized, the following rules apply:

(a) Never place sensitive data on removable media without specific authorization from the relevant System Owner or Head of Department;

(b) Authorized removable media containing sensitive data must be encrypted and kept in a secure location;

(c) Never use removable media to bring executable programs from outside the network without prior authorization and scanning with approved, up-to-date antivirus and anti-malware tools;

(d) All programs brought into the network must be on the IT Department's list of approved software.

### 9.5 Lost or Stolen Devices

9.5.1 Users must report lost or stolen organizational devices (computers, laptops, mobile phones, storage media, access cards) to their supervisor and the IT Department **immediately**.

9.5.2 The IT Department shall take immediate steps to remotely disable, lock, or wipe the device and revoke any associated credentials.

### 9.6 Equipment Transfer

9.6.1 IT equipment must not be transferred between employees, locations, or departments without authorization and completion of the **Asset Transfer Checklist** (see Section 24 and Appendix C).

### 9.7 Software Installation

9.7.1 Users must not install any unauthorized applications, software, plugins, or tools on organizational devices.

9.7.2 All software installations must be performed or approved by the IT Department (see Section 10).

### 9.8 Approved Applications

9.8.1 All employees may operate only programs on the IT Department's approved application list.

9.8.2 If an employee requires an application not on the approved list, they must submit the application to the IT Department for evaluation and approval prior to installation.

9.8.3 If an employee causes a security problem by installing or running an unapproved program, they risk disciplinary action.

9.8.4 The current list of approved applications includes (but is not limited to):

(a) Microsoft Office Suite (Word, Excel, PowerPoint, Outlook)

(b) Adobe Acrobat Reader

(c) Core Banking System (Finfinancials or approved successor)

(d) Approved communication/collaboration tools

(e) Approved web browsers (Microsoft Edge, Google Chrome – latest versions)

(f) Approved antivirus/endpoint protection software

(g) Approved compression utilities (e.g., WinRAR, 7-Zip)

(h) Approved remote support tools (as authorized by IT)

(i) Approved report-writing tools (e.g., Crystal Reports)

(j) Any other applications explicitly added to the approved list by the IT Department

9.8.5 Exceptions for testing or development may be granted to specific personnel as documented in Section 10.4.

## 10. SOFTWARE MANAGEMENT

### 10.1 Licensing and Legality

10.1.1 Only licensed and approved software may be installed on Ufulu Finance systems.

10.1.2 Pirated, illegally obtained, cracked, or unlicensed software is strictly prohibited.

10.1.3 Software installations must be performed or approved by the IT Department.

### 10.2 Software Inventory

10.2.1 The IT Department shall maintain a **Software Inventory** recording:

(a) Software name and version;

(b) License type and license key;

(c) Number of licenses purchased and allocated;

(d) Installation location (device/user);

(e) Vendor and support agreement details;

(f) Renewal/expiration dates.

10.2.2 The Software Inventory shall be reviewed at least annually for compliance.

### 10.3 Updates and Patching

10.3.1 All software must be regularly updated and patched in accordance with the **System Update Policy** (see Section 10.7 below and Section 22 for Change Management).

10.3.2 Unsupported or end-of-life software should be identified and removed or replaced in a timely manner.

### 10.4 Exceptions

Special exceptions to the approved software list may be made for specific employees depending on required job function and skills, including:

(a) Personnel who need to test new applications in a test environment before deployment;

(b) Developers who must run applications they have developed for testing purposes;

(c) Network/system administrators who need to operate and evaluate new software.

All exceptions must be documented and approved by the ICT Manager.

### 10.5 System Update Policy

10.5.1 **Update Types** – The following components shall be considered for updates:

(a) Computer BIOS/UEFI firmware;

(b) Operating systems (servers and workstations);

(c) Application software;

(d) Core banking application updates (e.g., Finfinancials);

(e) Core banking parameter/configuration updates;

(f) Security patches and anti-malware definitions.

10.5.2 **Update Identification** – Updates shall be identified through:

(a) Review of vendor security bulletins and patch advisories;

(b) Automatic scanning of systems for missing updates;

(c) Policy or regulatory changes requiring system parameter adjustments.

10.5.3 **Update Assessment** – Before applying updates, administrators shall determine:

(a) The vulnerability or issue addressed;

(b) Prerequisite patches or updates required;

(c) Programs and services affected;

(d) Potential risks or breakages;

(e) Rollback/undo procedure.

10.5.4 **Testing** – New patches and updates should be tested in a controlled test environment before deployment to production. A verified backup must exist before applying any update.

10.5.5 **Server Updates** – Server updates shall be performed by a qualified and authorized system administrator. Updates shall be checked no less than **monthly**.

10.5.6 **Workstation Updates** – Workstation updates shall be managed centrally (e.g., via Windows Server Update Services or an approved patch management tool). Available updates shall be reviewed **weekly**. Updates shall be applied to the test environment 2–3 days before organization-wide deployment.

10.5.7 **Documentation** – Each server shall have documentation including installed applications and patch history. All patches applied shall be documented.

## 12. EMAIL & COMMUNICATION

### 12.1 Official Use

12.1.1 Organizational email accounts are provided for official business communications.

12.1.2 Employees must use their Ufulu Finance email address for all business-related correspondence.

12.1.3 Organizational email accounts must not be used to register for unauthorized external services, mailing lists, or personal accounts.

### 12.2 Prohibited Email Activities

Users must not:

(a) Send passwords, credentials, or sensitive authentication information through ordinary unencrypted email;

(b) Open suspicious email attachments or click on suspicious links, particularly from unknown or unexpected senders;

(c) Forward chain letters, hoaxes, or unverified warnings;

(d) Send or forward offensive, harassing, discriminatory, or inappropriate content;

(e) Use email for unauthorized commercial or personal purposes;

(f) Impersonate another person or misrepresent their identity in email;

(g) Send confidential or sensitive information to unintended recipients without appropriate safeguards.

### 12.3 Phishing and Social Engineering

12.3.1 Users must be vigilant against phishing emails, spoofed sender addresses, and social engineering attacks.

12.3.2 Suspicious emails, particularly those requesting credentials, payment details, or urgent financial action, must be reported to the IT Department immediately.

12.3.3 Users must verify unexpected payment instructions or sensitive requests received via email through an independent secondary channel (e.g., phone call to the known contact) before acting.

### 12.4 Email Retention and Archiving

12.4.1 Email retention shall comply with Ufulu Finance's records management and regulatory requirements.

12.4.2 The IT Department may implement email archiving solutions for compliance and business continuity purposes.

### 12.5 Communication Tools

12.5.1 Only IT-approved communication and collaboration tools may be used for business purposes.

12.5.2 Sensitive or confidential information must not be shared through unauthorized messaging platforms, social media, or personal email accounts.

## 13. DATA PROTECTION & CONFIDENTIALITY

### 13.1 Data Classification

13.1.1 All organizational information shall be classified according to its sensitivity:

| Classification | Description | Handling Requirements |
|---|---|---|
| **Public** | Information approved for public release | No special restrictions |
| **Internal** | General operational information for internal use | Access limited to employees; not for external distribution |
| **Confidential** | Member data, financial records, loan information, employee records | Access restricted to authorized personnel; encryption required in transit and at rest where feasible; no copying to personal devices without authorization |
| **Highly Confidential / Restricted** | Authentication credentials, system configurations, audit data, strategic documents | Strictly limited access on a need-to-know basis; encrypted storage; enhanced monitoring |

### 13.2 Confidentiality Obligations

13.2.1 Confidential and sensitive information must only be accessible to authorized personnel with a legitimate business need.

13.2.2 Member/customer personal information must not be copied to personal devices, personal cloud storage, or personal email accounts without explicit authorization.

13.2.3 Sensitive documents must not be shared through unauthorized cloud storage services, messaging platforms, or personal communication channels.

### 13.3 Encryption

13.3.1 Sensitive data must be encrypted during transmission over networks (e.g., using TLS/SSL, VPN).

13.3.2 Sensitive data stored on portable devices, removable media, or transmitted externally must be encrypted.

13.3.3 Full disk encryption shall be enabled on all laptops and mobile devices used for Ufulu Finance business.

### 13.4 Data Protection Compliance

13.4.1 Personal data must be collected, processed, stored, and disposed of in accordance with applicable data protection legislation and Ufulu Finance's data protection procedures.

13.4.2 Member data shall be used only for the purposes for which it was collected and for which consent was obtained.

### 13.5 Secure Data Disposal

13.5.1 Data must be securely destroyed when retention requirements expire.

13.5.2 The level of data sanitization shall correspond to the data classification:

| Sensitivity Level | Disposal Method |
|---|---|
| **Unclassified / Public** | Standard deletion or reformatting is acceptable |
| **Internal / Sensitive** | Secure erasure, reformatting, or degaussing |
| **Confidential** | Approved secure erasure technology ensuring data is unrecoverable |
| **Highly Confidential / Restricted** | Approved secure erasure or physical destruction of media |

13.5.3 Approved data sanitization methods shall be specified in a **Media Data Removal Procedure** document by asset type (hard drives, flash disks, CDs/DVDs, tapes, mobile devices).

13.5.4 Certificates of data destruction shall be obtained for third-party disposal services.

## 16. FINANCIAL SYSTEM CONTROLS

### 16.1 Overview

Given the critical nature of Ufulu Finance's financial operations, the following enhanced controls apply to the core banking system (Finfinancials or successor), accounting systems, loan management systems, and any other systems processing financial transactions.

### 16.2 Role-Based Access

16.2.1 Users must have role-based permissions within financial systems, aligned with their job responsibilities.

16.2.2 System administrators shall not automatically have unrestricted financial transaction processing privileges.

### 16.3 Audit Trail

16.3.1 All financial transactions must have a complete and tamper-resistant audit trail recording:

(a) The user who initiated the transaction;

(b) The date and time of the transaction;

(c) The nature and details of the transaction;

(d) Any approvals or authorizations;

(e) Any subsequent modifications.

### 16.4 Transaction Integrity

16.4.1 Deleted financial transactions should preferably be **voided or reversed** rather than permanently deleted, to preserve the audit trail.

16.4.2 Changes to posted or finalized transactions must be logged and authorized.

16.4.3 Backdated transactions should be restricted and require specific authorization.

### 16.5 Maker-Checker Controls

16.5.1 Maker-checker (dual control) procedures must be implemented for sensitive transactions, including but not limited to:

(a) Large-value disbursements;

(b) Journal entries and adjustments;

(c) Changes to member account details;

(d) Loan approvals and modifications;

(e) Changes to interest rates, fees, or charges;

(f) System parameter changes.

16.5.2 The maker and checker must be different individuals.

### 16.6 Approval Workflows

16.6.1 Approval workflows must be implemented for sensitive transactions as defined by management and the system configuration.

16.6.2 Approval limits and hierarchies must be documented and reviewed periodically.

### 16.7 Period Locking

16.7.1 Financial periods must be capable of being locked after financial closing to prevent unauthorized post-closing entries.

16.7.2 Re-opening a closed period must require senior management authorization and be logged.

### 16.8 Bank Reconciliations

16.8.1 Bank reconciliations must be performed regularly and must have a documented audit history.

16.8.2 Reconciliation processes and results must be reviewable by Internal Audit.

### 16.9 Balance Traceability

16.9.1 Changes to account balances must be traceable to specific transactions.

16.9.2 Unexplained balance adjustments must be investigated and documented.

### 16.10 Reporting

16.10.1 Critical financial reports must identify the user who generated the report, and the date and time of generation.

16.10.2 Access to sensitive reports must be restricted to authorized personnel.

### 16.11 Segregation of Duties

16.11.1 Segregation of duties must be maintained within financial systems to prevent fraud and error.

16.11.2 No single individual should have end-to-end control over a significant financial process without independent oversight.

## 17. BACKUP & RECOVERY

### 17.1 Purpose

This policy is designed to protect Ufulu Finance's data to ensure it is not lost and can be recovered in the event of equipment failure, intentional destruction, disaster, or any other cause.

### 17.2 Scope

This section applies to all equipment and data owned and operated by Ufulu Finance.

### 17.3 Definitions

(a) **Backup** – The saving of files, databases, and system states onto secure storage media for the purpose of preventing data loss;

(b) **Archive** – The saving of old or unused files onto storage media for the purpose of releasing online storage space;

(c) **Restore** – The process of recovering data from backup media and returning it to the operational system.

### 17.4 What Must Be Backed Up

The following data and systems must be included in the backup program:

(a) Core banking system databases (Finfinancials live database);

(b) Financial accounting databases;

(c) User work-related data stored on network drives and servers;

(d) System state data and server configurations;

(e) Registry and configuration files;

(f) Application files and settings;

(g) Email systems;

(h) Network device configurations (firewalls, routers, switches);

(i) Any other data identified as critical by the IT Department or management.

### 17.5 Backup Schedule

| Backup Type | Frequency | Timing |
|---|---|---|
| Full Database Backup (Core Banking System) | Daily | 16:40 hours, Monday to Friday |
| Full System/File Backup | Daily | 16:40 hours, Monday to Friday |
| Incremental/Differential Backup | As determined by IT | Per IT Department schedule |
| Weekly Full Backup | Weekly (Friday) | Scheduled after close of business |
| Monthly Full Backup / Archive | Monthly | First weekend of each month |

If backups are not performed on Friday due to maintenance, they shall be completed on Saturday or Sunday.

### 17.6 Backup Responsibility

17.6.1 The IT Officer or a delegated and trained member of staff shall perform regular backups.

17.6.2 The IT Department shall develop and maintain documented backup procedures.

### 17.7 3-2-1 Backup Principle

For critical systems, Ufulu Finance shall apply the **3-2-1 backup principle**:

- **3 copies** of all critical data (1 production copy + 2 backup copies);
- **2 different storage types** (e.g., disk and tape, or local disk and cloud);
- **1 copy stored off-site** at a physically separate, secure location.

### 17.8 Backup Storage Locations

17.8.1 Offline/daily backup media shall be stored in Ufulu Finance's fireproof safe.

17.8.2 A second copy shall be stored at an approved off-site location identified by management.

17.8.3 Monthly archive media shall be stored at a designated off-site secure facility (e.g., MUSCCO's offices or another approved location) in a fireproof safe.

17.8.4 If cloud-based backup is used, the cloud provider must be approved by the IT Department and must meet Ufulu Finance's security and confidentiality requirements.

### 17.9 Backup Testing and Restoration

17.9.1 The ability to restore data from backups shall be tested **at least once per month**.

17.9.2 The IT Officer shall develop a procedure for testing backups and document the results of each test.

17.9.3 An annual comprehensive disaster recovery test shall include full restoration of critical systems from backup.

### 17.10 Restoration Requests

17.10.1 Users requiring file restoration must submit a request to the IT Help Desk, including:

(a) File creation date;

(b) File name;

(c) Last modification date;

(d) Date and time the file was deleted or lost;

(e) Location where the file was stored.

17.10.2 Restoration requests shall be prioritized based on business criticality.

### 17.11 Backup Security

17.11.1 Backup media must be protected against unauthorized access, theft, and environmental damage.

17.11.2 Backup data containing sensitive information must be encrypted.

17.11.3 Access to backup systems and media must be restricted to authorized IT personnel.

## 18. DISASTER RECOVERY

### 18.1 Purpose

Ufulu Finance shall maintain a documented **Disaster Recovery Plan (DRP)** to ensure the timely restoration of critical IT systems and business operations following a disruption.

### 18.2 Disaster Scenarios

The DRP shall address, at a minimum, the following scenarios:

(a) Server failure or corruption;

(b) Database corruption or failure;

(c) Malware, ransomware, or cyberattack;

(d) Hardware failure;

(e) Power failure or outage;

(f) Internet or network connectivity failure;

(g) Accidental or malicious data deletion;

(h) Fire, flood, or physical disaster;

(i) Loss of key IT personnel;

(j) Vendor or service provider failure.

### 18.3 Recovery Objectives

| System | Recovery Time Objective (RTO) | Recovery Point Objective (RPO) |
|---|---|---|
| Core Banking System (Finfinancials) | ≤ 4 hours | ≤ 1 hour (last daily backup) |
| File Server | ≤ 8 hours | ≤ 24 hours |
| Email Server | ≤ 8 hours | ≤ 24 hours |
| Network Infrastructure | ≤ 4 hours | N/A |
| Accounting/Financial Systems | ≤ 8 hours | ≤ 24 hours |
| Other Systems | ≤ 24 hours | ≤ 24 hours |

*Note: RTO and RPO values should be reviewed and adjusted annually based on business requirements and risk assessment.*

### 18.4 DRP Components

The Disaster Recovery Plan shall include:

(a) Recovery priorities and sequencing;

(b) Responsible personnel and their deputies;

(c) Backup locations and access procedures;

(d) Emergency contact list (internal and external);

(e) Step-by-step recovery procedures for each critical system;

(f) Communication plan (internal and external);

(g) Alternative processing arrangements;

(h) Testing frequency and results documentation;

(i) Post-recovery review and lessons learned process.

### 18.5 DRP Testing

18.5.1 The DRP shall be tested at least **annually** through a tabletop exercise or a full simulation.

18.5.2 Test results shall be documented and any identified gaps shall be addressed.

### 18.6 Critical Equipment Redundancy

All critical services are required to utilize redundant technologies including:

(a) Dual power supplies on all servers providing critical services;

(b) RAID disk arrays to prevent a single disk failure from interrupting services;

(c) Uninterruptible Power Supplies (UPS) capable of providing power for a minimum of **1 hour** to servers operating critical services in the event of a power outage;

(d) For services that are critical for income or operations and cannot be interrupted, the following are also recommended:

   (i) A backup generator to ensure long-term power outages do not interrupt service;

   (ii) More than one server for the same service using clustering or load-balancing technology.

### 18.7 Critical Services

Critical services required for normal operation include:

(a) File sharing services on file servers;

(b) Web services;

(c) Email services;

(d) Database services for internal users and critical external applications;

(e) Critical external application servers;

(f) Domain controller servers;

(g) Firewall and network connectivity services;

(h) Core banking application services.

## 23. IT INCIDENT MANAGEMENT

### 23.1 Definition of an Incident

An incident is any event that disrupts or threatens the confidentiality, integrity, or availability of Ufulu Finance's IT systems, data, or services. Incidents include but are not limited to:

(a) Loss of information confidentiality (data theft or breach);

(b) Compromise of information integrity (unauthorized data modification);

(c) Theft of physical IT assets;

(d) Damage to physical IT assets;

(e) Denial of service;

(f) Misuse of services, information, or assets;

(g) Infection of systems by malware, ransomware, or hostile software;

(h) Attempted unauthorized access;

(i) Unauthorized changes to hardware, software, or configuration;

(j) Reports of unusual system behavior;

(k) Intrusion detection alarms;

(l) System failures or outages;

(m) Incorrect financial transactions due to system errors;

(n) Database problems;

(o) Network outages.

### 23.2 Reporting Obligations

23.2.1 All employees must promptly report any of the following to their supervisor and the IT Department:

(a) System failures or errors;

(b) Suspected malware or virus infections;

(c) Suspicious emails or phishing attempts;

(d) Suspected unauthorized access;

(e) Lost or stolen devices;

(f) Suspected data leaks or breaches;

(g) Incorrect financial transactions;

(h) Application errors;

(i) Database problems;

(j) Network outages;

(k) Any other IT security concern.

23.2.2 Any employee aware of any violation of this POLICY is required to report it to their supervisor or the IT Department.

### 23.3 IT Incident Register

The IT Department shall maintain an **IT Incident Register** with the following fields:

| Field | Example |
|---|---|
| Incident ID | INC-2026-00125 |
| Date/Time Reported | 13-Aug-2026 10:30 |
| Reported By | Finance Department |
| System Affected | Core Banking System |
| Severity | Critical / High / Medium / Low |
| Description | Users unable to post transactions |
| Assigned To | IT Support |
| Actions Taken | Database service restarted |
| Root Cause | Server resource exhaustion |
| Downtime | 35 minutes |
| Resolution | Service restored; server resources increased |
| Status | Open / In Progress / Resolved / Closed |

### 23.4 Incident Response Life Cycle

The incident response process shall follow these phases:

(a) **Preparation** – Maintain policies, procedures, tools, contacts, and training;

(b) **Discovery** – Detect and identify the incident through monitoring, alerts, or user reports;

(c) **Notification** – Activate the emergency contact procedure and notify the incident response team;

(d) **Analysis and Assessment** – Determine whether the incident is real, its scope, impact, and urgency;

(e) **Containment** – Take immediate action to prevent further damage (disconnect systems, change passwords, block connections);

(f) **Eradication and Prevention of Re-infection** – Determine the root cause and take steps to prevent recurrence (patch systems, close ports, remove malware, re-install systems);

(g) **Recovery** – Restore affected systems to normal operation, verify integrity, and restore data from clean backups;

(h) **Documentation** – Document all aspects of the incident, response, and outcome;

(i) **Evidence Preservation** – Preserve logs, emails, and other evidence for investigation and potential legal action;

(j) **Damage Assessment** – Assess the impact and cost of the incident and the response;

(k) **Post-Incident Review** – Review the response, update policies and procedures, and implement lessons learned.

### 23.5 Incident Severity Levels

| Severity | Description | Response Time |
|---|---|---|
| **Critical** | Complete loss of critical systems; data breach; financial fraud | Immediate |
| **High** | Significant degradation of critical services; potential data compromise | Within 1 hour |
| **Medium** | Limited impact on services; non-critical system issues | Within 4 hours |
| **Low** | Minor issues with minimal business impact | Within 24 hours |

### 23.6 Escalation

23.6.1 Critical and high-severity incidents must be escalated to senior management promptly.

23.6.2 Incidents involving potential data breaches, financial fraud, or criminal activity must be reported to the appropriate authorities as required by law.

25.5.1 Third-party personnel working on Ufulu Finance systems should be supervised by an Ufulu Finance IT staff member where feasible.

---

## 26. CLOUD SERVICES

### 26.1 Approval

26.1.1 The use of cloud services (SaaS, PaaS, IaaS) for Ufulu Finance business purposes must be approved by the IT Department.

26.1.2 Unauthorized use of personal cloud storage accounts (e.g., personal Dropbox, Google Drive, OneDrive) for storing organizational data is prohibited.

### 26.2 Security Requirements

26.2.1 Approved cloud service providers must meet Ufulu Finance's security, confidentiality, and data protection requirements.

26.2.2 Data stored in the cloud must be encrypted in transit and, where feasible, at rest.

26.2.3 Access to cloud services must be controlled through strong authentication, including MFA where available.

### 26.3 Data Residency and Compliance

26.3.1 The IT Department must ensure that the use of cloud services complies with applicable data protection laws and regulatory requirements regarding data residency and cross-border data transfers.

### 26.4 Vendor Assessment

26.4.1 Before adopting a cloud service, the IT Department must assess the provider's security practices, data protection commitments, service continuity, and exit strategy.

---

## 27. BUSINESS CONTINUITY

### 27.1 Integration with DRP

27.1.1 IT disaster recovery is a component of Ufulu Finance's overall **Business Continuity Plan (BCP)**.

27.1.2 The BCP shall ensure that critical business functions can continue during and after a major disruption.

### 27.2 Business Impact Analysis

27.2.1 A Business Impact Analysis (BIA) shall be conducted periodically to identify critical business processes, their IT dependencies, and acceptable downtime.

### 27.3 Alternative Processing

27.3.1 Manual or alternative processing procedures shall be documented for critical operations in the event of prolonged system unavailability.

### 27.4 Communication Plan

27.4.1 A communication plan shall be maintained for notifying employees, members, regulators, and other stakeholders in the event of a significant disruption.

## 28. IT AUDIT & MONITORING

### 28.1 Periodic Reviews

The following shall be periodically reviewed by the IT Department and/or Internal Audit:

(a) User accounts and access rights;

(b) Privileged and administrator accounts;

(c) System and security logs;

(d) Financial transaction audit trails;

(e) Database activity logs;

(f) Backup status and restoration test results;

(g) Security incidents and trends;

(h) Software license compliance;

(i) IT Asset Register accuracy;

(j) Network activity and performance;

(k) Change management records;

(l) Compliance with this POLICY.

### 28.2 Audit Logs

28.2.1 Critical systems must maintain tamper-resistant audit logs.

28.2.2 Audit logs must be retained for a period consistent with regulatory requirements and Ufulu Finance's records management policy.

28.2.3 Access to audit logs must be restricted to authorized personnel.

### 28.3 Internal Audit Role

28.3.1 Internal Audit shall include IT controls and cybersecurity in its annual audit plan.

28.3.2 Internal Audit shall have unrestricted access to IT records, logs, and systems necessary to perform its function.

28.3.3 Findings and recommendations from IT audits shall be tracked to resolution.

## 29. EMPLOYEE ONBOARDING & OFFBOARDING

### 29.1 Onboarding

When a new employee joins Ufulu Finance, the following IT-related steps must be completed:

(a) Create an approved user account with a unique user ID;

(b) Assign appropriate roles and access rights based on the employee's position and job requirements;

(c) Provide required IT equipment (computer, phone, access cards, etc.) and record in the IT Asset Register;

(d) Issue and have the employee sign the Memory Device Trustee Agreement (if applicable);

(e) Provide IT security awareness training covering this POLICY, password rules, email security, and data handling;

(f) Obtain the employee's written acknowledgement of this POLICY and all related IT procedures;

(g) Set initial password with mandatory change at first login;

(h) Configure email account and approved applications.

### 29.2 Offboarding

When an employee leaves Ufulu Finance (resignation, termination, retirement, or contract expiry), the following steps must be completed:

(a) Disable or remove all user accounts on or before the employee's last working day;

(b) Revoke access to all systems, applications, and data;

(c) Disable email access;

(d) Revoke VPN and remote access;

(e) Revoke cloud service access;

(f) Remove access to financial and core banking systems;

(g) Recover all IT equipment and record in the Asset Register;

(h) Ensure the employee returns all removable media and storage devices;

(i) Transfer ownership of organizational files and documents as needed;

(j) Change any shared or administrative credentials to which the employee had access;

(k) Document completion of the offboarding checklist.

### 29.3 Role Changes and Transfers

29.3.1 When an employee changes roles or transfers to a different department, access rights must be reviewed and adjusted accordingly.

29.3.2 The Head of Department must notify the IT Department of role changes promptly.

## 30. COMPUTER & SECURITY TRAINING

### 30.1 Purpose

Training is essential to protect Ufulu Finance's resources, increase employee efficiency, and ensure that all users are aware of IT security threats and their responsibilities under this POLICY.

### 30.2 Training Scope

All Ufulu Finance employees shall complete IT security awareness training. Training categories shall include but not be limited to:

**Basics:**

(a) File management, storage, and organization;

(b) Understanding file extensions and security implications;

(c) Network drive usage;

(d) Email attachment handling;

(e) Efficiency tools and keyboard shortcuts.

**Core Banking System:**

(a) Proper use of Finfinancials or the approved core banking system.

**Malware Awareness:**

(a) How malware spreads (email, browsers, removable media, unapproved software);

(b) Email viruses, spoofing, and dangerous attachments;

(c) Email SPAM protection and filtering;

(d) Hoaxes, phishing, and fraud methods.

**Email Use:**

(a) Out-of-office replies;

(b) Mail filtering rules;

(c) Recognizing and reporting suspicious emails.

**Web Browser Security:**

(a) Safe browsing practices;

(b) Avoiding adware, spyware, and malicious websites;

(c) Browser security settings;

(d) Approved security tools.

**Password Security:**

(a) Importance of password protection;

(b) Password change requirements;

(c) Creating strong, memorable passwords;

(d) Risks of password exposure.

**Other Security Topics:**

(a) Firewall awareness;

(b) Malware risks;

(c) Vulnerability awareness;

(d) Social engineering recognition and prevention;

(e) Data protection and confidentiality;

(f) Incident reporting procedures.

### 30.3 Training Delivery

(a) Scheduled training seminars (1–4 hours);

(b) Brown bag lunch training sessions (up to 1 hour, 1–2 days per week);

(c) Online training modules (where available);

(d) Phishing simulation exercises;

(e) New employee induction training;

(f) Annual security refresher training.

### 30.4 Requirements

30.4.1 All Ufulu Finance staff shall make measurable and continuous progress in the training areas listed above.

30.4.2 Each Head of Department shall be responsible for ensuring that employees under their supervision complete required training.

30.4.3 Each employee must complete foundational IT security training within the first **three (3) months** of employment.

30.4.4 Annual refresher training is mandatory for all employees.

### 30.5 Assessment

30.5.1 Random assessments may be used to verify that training policy is being followed.

30.5.2 IT personnel may test employees at random about their knowledge of security topics.

## 31. POLICY VIOLATIONS & DISCIPLINARY MEASURES

### 31.1 Enforcement

31.1.1 Compliance with this POLICY is mandatory for all employees and authorized users.

31.1.2 Since IT security, data integrity, and resource protection are critical to the operation of Ufulu Finance, violations of this POLICY may result in disciplinary action.

### 31.2 Disciplinary Process

31.2.1 Alleged violations shall be investigated in accordance with Ufulu Finance's disciplinary procedures and applicable employment law.

31.2.2 Disciplinary measures may include, depending on the severity of the violation:

(a) Verbal warning;

(b) Written warning;

(c) Suspension;

(d) Termination of employment;

(e) Legal action and/or reporting to law enforcement authorities.

### 31.3 Reporting Violations

31.3.1 Any employee aware of any violation of this POLICY is required to report it to their supervisor, the IT Department, or other authorized representative.

31.3.2 Ufulu Finance shall not retaliate against employees who report violations in good faith.

### 31.4 Third-Party Violations

31.4.1 Violations by third-party personnel, contractors, or vendors may result in termination of access, termination of contract, and/or legal action.

---

## 32. EXCEPTIONS

### 32.1 Exception Process

32.1.1 Any exception to this POLICY must be requested in writing to the ICT Manager, documenting:

(a) The specific policy requirement from which an exception is sought;

(b) The business justification for the exception;

(c) The proposed duration of the exception;

(d) The risk assessment and mitigating controls.

32.1.2 Exceptions must be approved by the ICT Manager and, for high-risk exceptions, by senior management.

32.1.3 All approved exceptions must be documented and reviewed periodically.

32.1.4 Exceptions shall not be granted where they would violate applicable law or regulation.
