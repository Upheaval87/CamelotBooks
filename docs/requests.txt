Here's the idea I'd build in your position: **a productized AI automation service that turns into a niche SaaS.**

## The idea

Pick one narrow industry where small businesses drown in repetitive admin, such as property managers, dental clinics, accounting firms, or home-service contractors. Sell them one specific outcome, like "every missed call and web inquiry gets answered, qualified, and booked automatically in under 60 seconds."

The pattern is **services first, product second**:

1. **Months 1-3: sell it as a done-for-you service.** You build the automation with tools like Claude's API, n8n or Make, and a voice or SMS provider, then set it up for each client. Charge a setup fee ($1,500-3,000) plus $500-1,500/month.
2. **Months 4-9: standardize.** Every client in the niche wants roughly the same thing, so you template it and cut setup time from days to hours.
3. **Months 10-18: turn it into software.** Wrap the template in a simple dashboard with self-serve onboarding, and move to $300-800/month subscriptions.

## Why this works for you

- **It costs almost nothing to start.** Free tiers and a $20-50/month tool stack are enough to build and demo, and clients pay you before you've spent much.
- **Your technical skill is the moat.** Most small businesses can't wire AI into their calendar, CRM, and phone system, so they pay for someone who can.
- **A narrow niche makes sales easy.** "I help dental clinics stop losing patients to missed calls" gets replies. "I build AI automations" gets ignored.

## The math to $1M

| Path | Calculation |
|---|---|
| Service-heavy | 40 clients × $2,000/month × 12 = **$960K/year** |
| SaaS-leaning | 200 customers × $400/month × 12 = **$960K/year** |
| Hybrid | 15 retainer clients ($30K/month) + 100 SaaS users ($40K/month) = **$840K/year** |

Realistically, month 12 revenue of $20-40K/month is achievable if you land clients consistently. The $1M run rate typically arrives in year 2.

## Your first 30 days

- **Week 1:** Choose your niche. Pick an industry where missed leads cost real money, and where you can find 100 prospects on Google Maps or LinkedIn.
- **Week 2:** Build a working demo, such as an AI that answers a sample clinic's inquiries by text or voice, and record a 2-minute video of it working.
- **Weeks 3-4:** Contact 20-30 prospects a day by email, DM, and phone. Offer a free 7-day pilot in exchange for a testimonial. Aim for 3 paying clients by day 30.

## Biggest risks

- **Picking too broad a niche.** Resist serving everyone.
- **Building before selling.** Don't write production code until someone has agreed to pay.
- **Churn.** Small businesses leave fast if they can't see results, so send a simple monthly report showing leads captured and revenue recovered.
